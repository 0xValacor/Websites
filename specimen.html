Specimen of the week

Acanthometra

a star made of stone, smaller than a grain of sand

Acantharians are single-celled plankton that build their skeletons from strontium sulfate, a mineral almost nothing else uses. Exactly twenty spines meet at the centre in a fixed geometry first described by Johannes Müller in 1858.
