01 — THE LAST SUMMER

Earth didn't end.
It just stopped needing us.

The oceans rose politely. The cities dimmed one grid at a time. By the time the final census came back, there were more satellites in the sky than lit windows on the ground.

We built the Ark in orbit over thirty years, from the bones of old space stations and the steel of a world that no longer used it. Nobody called it an escape. We called it a departure, the way you call it a departure when you leave a house you grew up in.
